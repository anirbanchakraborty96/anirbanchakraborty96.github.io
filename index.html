Research

Measuring the brain's response to an implanted electrode

Implanted microelectrodes lose recording quality over weeks to months, and the surrounding tissue response is a substantial part of the reason. My work asks which cell types change, which genes drive those changes, and how far the effect extends from the device.

I build analysis pipelines for 10x Genomics Visium and Xenium data, apply statistical and network models across cell types, implant materials, brain regions and time points, and turn the results into tools experimental researchers can use directly.

Tissue response

Ongoing

Single-cell-resolution comparison of the response to silicon and polyimide electrodes across acute to chronic time points.

SpatiaXen

Software · published

A no-code platform for cell-type-resolved analysis of Xenium data, so researchers can explore their own slides without building a pipeline.

DiffCoRank

Method · published

Identifies hub genes and differential co-expression networks, ranking the molecular drivers of the implant response rather than eyeballing them.
